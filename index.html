How to play

  1. Press START. You have 240 seconds for the whole wheel.
  2. For each letter a song plays for 5 seconds. Its title starts with that letter, or contains it when the hint says so.
  3. Type the title and hit Enter. Spelling does not need to be perfect.
  4. Not sure? Press PASS (or Esc). The letter comes back on the next lap and its 5 seconds play once more.
  5. Name all the songs before the clock runs out to complete the wheel.

Wheels: GENERAL mixes every decade; 70s to 10s stick to one. Each has a new wheel every day, the same for everybody, and one attempt.

Unlimited: no wheel. Songs keep coming, 25 seconds each, until you make 3 mistakes. Play it as often as you like.
