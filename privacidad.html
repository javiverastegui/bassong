What the game stores

AlphaBeat needs no account. It keeps your progress for today's wheel, your hi-score and your sound setting in your browser's local storage. None of it is sent to a server.

Song previews

Song snippets and cover art are loaded from Apple's iTunes servers, which receive your IP address as with any web request. Previews are provided courtesy of iTunes.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Ads and tracking

AlphaBeat shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking cookies.
