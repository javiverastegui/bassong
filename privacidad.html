What the game stores

Bassong needs no account. It keeps your progress on today's wheels, your best unlimited run and your settings (sound, volume, mode and language) in your browser's local storage. None of it is sent to a server.

Song previews

Song snippets and cover art are loaded from Apple's iTunes servers, which receive your IP address as with any web request. Previews are provided courtesy of iTunes.

Fonts

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Ads and tracking

Bassong shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking cookies.
